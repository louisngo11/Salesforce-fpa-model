Growth has slowed to high single digits. Margins and cash flow now carry the story.

Revenue nearly doubled from FY21 to FY26 while GAAP operating margin rose from 2% to 20%. The forecast tests how much of that margin survives a slower top line.

  • FY26 revenue beat plan by $825M. $41.5B against a $40.7B guidance midpoint, partly from the Informatica acquisition.
  • FY26 GAAP margin missed plan by 1.5 points. 20.1% against a 21.6% guide, as restructuring and deal costs absorbed the upside.
  • FY27 is being reforecast up on revenue, down on margin. The revenue midpoint is up $250M since February, mostly acquired, while the GAAP margin guide fell from 20.9% to 20.1%.
Source: SEC EDGAR XBRL, 10-K and 10-Q filings

Method

  • Revenue. FY27E is the low, mid or high point of the August 2026 guidance range, depending on scenario. FY28E growth is an input. Each later year fades by the fade input. A macro adjustment adds (GDP assumption − 2.3%) × the elasticity input to every year from FY28 on.
  • Costs. FY27E ratios equal first-half FY27 actuals. FY28E–FY31E ratios move in a straight line toward the FY31 targets. Gross margin is held flat at the input.
  • Cash. Operating cash flow and capex are modeled as % of revenue. FCF = OCF − capex. Rule of 40 = revenue growth % + FCF margin %.
  • GAAP only. The forecast is GAAP and is not adjusted for stock-based compensation or amortization. Non-GAAP margins appear only where the company guided them.

Known gaps

  • FRED, BLS and World Bank APIs were unreachable from the build environment, so economic figures come from published releases instead of live series.
  • Segment and cloud-level revenue (Sales, Service, Platform, Integration and Analytics) isn't in the XBRL company facts. Adding it needs the 10-K segment tables.
  • FY27E includes Informatica and, after closing, the pending Contentful and Fin acquisitions. Organic growth is not split out.
